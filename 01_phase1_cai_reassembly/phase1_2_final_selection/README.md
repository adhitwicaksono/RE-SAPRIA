# Phase 1.2 — Cai Final Assembly Selection

## Objective

Select one manuscript-grade Cai assembly after comparing the unpolished draft and all polishing branches.

## Candidate naming

- `Cai_RE-SAPRIA_candidate_Flye_v1`
- `Cai_RE-SAPRIA_candidate_Pilon_r1`
- `Cai_RE-SAPRIA_candidate_Pilon_r2`
- `Cai_RE-SAPRIA_candidate_HyPo`
- final: `Cai_RE-SAPRIA_final_v1`

## Selection criteria

- highest defensible gene-space integrity;
- low internal-stop burden;
- stable assembly span;
- strong read support;
- no unexplained sequence loss;
- reproducible parameters;
- biologically plausible coverage;
- improved or preserved contiguity.

## Required final comparisons

1. final Cai vs published Cai;
2. final Cai vs published Guo;
3. final Cai self-control;
4. final Cai BUSCO using AUGUSTUS and Miniprot;
5. final Cai QUAST;
6. final Cai DNAdiff and MUMmerplot;
7. final Cai data and software manifest.

## Warning

A smaller assembly is not automatically more correct, and a larger assembly is not automatically more complete.
