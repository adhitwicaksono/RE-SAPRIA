# Phase 6 — Functional and Comparative Genomics

## Objective

Assign functional evidence to the selected structural annotations and compare conserved and divergent gene content between Cai and Guo.

## Candidate analyses

- DIAMOND protein similarity;
- InterProScan or Pfam domains;
- BUSCO and OMArk;
- OrthoFinder;
- pathway inference;
- gene-family summaries;
- intron and gene-structure profiling;
- characterization of annotation-only and assembly-only differences.

## Interpretation principles

- absence of annotation is not proof of gene absence;
- pathway inference depends on structural annotation quality;
- lineage-specific candidates require strong filters;
- apparent expansions must be checked for fragmented or repeat-derived models.
