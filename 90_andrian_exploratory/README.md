# Track 90 — Andrian Exploratory Internship

## Purpose

This track records Andrian's independent and supervised experiments with genome assembly and QC.

The internship is not a thesis project, and Andrian is not responsible for the manuscript's critical path.

## Learning objective

Learn how to distinguish:

- a completed computational job;
- a numerically attractive assembly;
- a biologically credible assembly;
- a manuscript-grade reproducible analysis.

## Permitted exploration

- Cai or Guo read subsets;
- full *Sapria* datasets where feasible;
- other genomes;
- alternative Flye parameters;
- read-depth experiments;
- QC tool comparisons;
- failed or resource-limited runs.

## Early-September presentation

The presentation should include:

1. research question;
2. datasets;
3. exact parameters;
4. successful and failed runs;
5. QUAST and BUSCO;
6. mapping or coverage QC where feasible;
7. troubleshooting;
8. lessons for bioinformatics consulting;
9. recommendations for future work.

## Boundary with the main analysis

Exploratory results remain here unless they are reproduced and formally migrated into the main phase folders.
